# Transfer market visual concepts

Three concepts generated with the built-in image generation tool. Source prompts are in prompts.md. No game code, APIs, balance, or saves were changed.

| Direction | Character appeal | Rarity readability | Information density | Relative implementation effort |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Evolved current | Expressive illustrated portraits, approachable and recognisable | Clear labels and colour; quietest treatment overall | Cleanest everyday scanning | Lowest of these three, though portraits still require a new modular art system |
| Playful pixel art | Strong indie-game personality and distinctive room details | Stepped frames and badges are clear; decorative pixels add noise | Same useful data, busier presentation | Medium to high: pixel character and equipment asset pipeline |
| Premium esports | Strong collectible appeal and polished portrait lighting | Most celebratory high-rarity treatment; low-tier metallic edges should be quieter | Same data, but reflective frames compete for attention | Highest: modular premium character artwork plus consistent materials and equipment |

Recommendation: use evolved current as the interface foundation, with pixel art if a playful idle-game identity is the priority. Premium framing could be borrowed sparingly for rare acquisitions.

Visual inspection: all six cards, signing prices, prize cuts, ratings, potentials, and the rarity legend fit. The three concepts preserve the same player data and ordering. Ratings deliberately overlap across rarity levels. No full stat grids obscure the purchase comparison.

Concept limitations: the generator delivered 1586 × 992 PNGs rather than the requested 1440 × 900. These are art-direction studies, not exact production specifications. Some decorative slogans persisted, pixel-style headings were introduced in the pixel option, and the Star/Superstar shape distinction is inconsistent in the first concept and premium legend. Production should standardise Star to four points and Superstar to five, remove slogans, retain smooth readable data typography, and reduce low-tier frame emphasis. Portrait cropping does not validate full-body silhouettes or modular customisation. Accessibility contrast and responsive behaviour require checks in the eventual implementation.
